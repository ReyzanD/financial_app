## 📊 **Chart Details**

### **Bar Chart (Spending Trend)**
**Purpose:** Show daily spending over last 7 days

**What You See:**
- Each bar = one day
- Bar height = spending amount
- Purple color = your theme
- Grid lines for easy reading

**How to Read:**
- Taller bars = more spending
- Compare bar heights to see spending patterns
- Check which days you spend most

### **Pie Chart (Category Breakdown)**
**Purpose:** Show spending distribution by category

**What You See:**
- Each slice = one category
- Slice size = percentage of total spending
- Different colors for each category
- Legend with category names

**How to Read:**
- Bigger slices = more spending in that category
- Check percentages to see proportions
- Identify your biggest expense categories

## 💡 **Use Cases**

### **1. Daily Spending Check**
**Goal:** See how much I spent today vs other days
- Select **"Minggu Ini"**
- Look at bar chart
- Compare today's bar with previous days

### **2. Find Biggest Expense Category**
**Goal:** Where does most of my money go?
- Look at **pie chart**
- Find the biggest slice
- Read the percentage

### **3. Monthly Progress**
**Goal:** Am I spending less this month?
- Look at **Monthly Comparison** section
- Check expense percentage change
- Green ↘️ = improvement (spending less)
- Red ↗️ = need attention (spending more)

### **4. Budget Planning**
**Goal:** Plan next month's budget
- Review **Category Breakdown**
- Identify high-spending categories
- Set budgets for each category

### **5. Savings Analysis**
**Goal:** How much am I saving?
- Check **Monthly Comparison**
- Look at "Savings" row
- See if savings increased or decreased
